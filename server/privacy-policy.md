# Zerostash Privacy Policy

**The Zerostash extension does not collect your data.** Nothing you do with it reaches the developer.

- It stores your server URL and access key locally in your browser (chrome.storage.local).
- When you save a bookmark, the page title, URL and metadata are sent only to the Zerostash server you configured — your own Cloudflare account.
- No analytics, no telemetry, no advertising, no third-party services.

The developer has no access to, and never receives, any user data. Data you save lives in your own Cloudflare account and is governed by your account's settings.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/jammutkarsh/zerostash
