Zerostash Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

The Zerostash extension sends what you save only to the Zerostash server you set it up with, which runs in your own Cloudflare account. The developer of Zerostash runs no service that receives, stores or sees your data. There is no analytics, tracking or advertising.

This policy covers the Zerostash browser extension (for Chrome and Firefox) and the Zerostash server software. Zerostash is open-source software (MIT licence); you deploy the server to your own Cloudflare account and connect the extension to it.

What the extension handles

  • Your settings: your server's address and your server secret, stored on your device in the browser's extension storage (chrome.storage.local). They are never synced or sent anywhere except to that server, to sign requests.
  • Pages you choose to save: when you click the toolbar button, use the keyboard shortcut or a right-click menu item, the extension reads the address (URL) and title of that page and its preview metadata (description, preview image address, site name, icon address and author, from the page's <head>). If you save a link or selected text from the right-click menu, it reads that link or that text. A note you type in the extension is sent with it.
  • Link previews (optional permission): if you grant the optional "all sites" permission, the extension fetches pages you have already saved that still have no preview, using your browser (so pages behind your logins work), reads the same preview metadata and sends it to your server. It does not read other pages, and does not collect your browsing history.
  • Your server's own pages: on the Zerostash web app's address only, a small script passes a "bookmark saved" notice from the web app to the extension so previews appear sooner. It reads nothing else.

All of this is sent only to the server address you entered, over HTTPS. The extension is used only to save and organise your bookmarks.

What Zerostash does not do

  • No data is sent to the developer of Zerostash or to any third party by the extension.
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, advertising or tracking.
  • Your data is not sold, shared for advertising, or used to judge creditworthiness or for any purpose other than the bookmark features you use.

The extension's use of information received from browser APIs complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Your server

Bookmarks, notes, tags, lists and uploaded files are stored in your own Cloudflare account (D1 database and R2 storage) and are subject to your account's settings and Cloudflare's terms. You control them: you can export or delete them at any time.

If you turn on optional features on your server, it sends data to the services you choose: AI tags and summaries send a saved page's address, title and text to the AI provider you configure (Cloudflare Workers AI by default, or your own Anthropic, OpenAI, Google or other provider key); RSS feeds are fetched from the addresses you subscribe to; webhooks send bookmark events to the addresses you enter. Those services handle data under their own policies.

Removing your data

Uninstalling the extension deletes its settings from your browser. Delete bookmarks in the Zerostash app, or delete the Zerostash Worker, database and storage bucket in your Cloudflare dashboard to remove everything.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page, with a new "last updated" date. Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
